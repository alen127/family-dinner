Family Dinner

A card game of passive aggression

Four courses. Four relatives. Keep your composure longer than they keep theirs.

  • Insults wear down their composure.
  • Deflects soak up what they throw back.
  • Schemes make them Flustered (they take 50% more) or you Smug (your insults hit harder).
  • You get 3 Spite to spend each turn. Tap a card to read it, tap again to say it.
